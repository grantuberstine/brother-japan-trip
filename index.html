Fuji / Hakone

Considering one of these · three ways to do it

Pick one, or skip Decide
Mountain day
Option A · Fuji day trip from Tokyo (Day 3)
Kawaguchiko (Fuji Five Lakes) — ~2 hr from Shinjuku by highway bus or the Fuji Excursion train. Chūreitō Pagoda (the pagoda-and-Fuji photo), Lake Kawaguchi, Oishi Park.
Closest, biggest Fuji views. Leave early — the mountain is clearest in the morning and often clouds over by afternoon. Late October is the start of clear-sky season.
Option B · Hakone day trip from Tokyo (Day 3)
Hakone loop — Romancecar from Shinjuku (85 min) or bullet train to Odawara (35 min). Hakone Free Pass covers the mountain train, ropeway over the volcanic valley, pirate ship across Lake Ashi, and the red torii in the water at Hakone Shrine.
More variety than Fuji, hot springs, Fuji in the background if it's clear. Long day: ~10 hours door to door.
Option C · Hakone on the way to Kyoto (Day 4, overnight)
Ship suitcases Tokyo → Kyoto, stop in Hakone for a night at a hot-spring ryokan with dinner, continue to Kyoto next morning (2 hr 10 from Odawara).
Takes one Kyoto night. The most relaxed version, and the only one with an onsen soak.

Rough days

A proposed split of the lists above · nothing fixed · move things around freely

Sunday, Oct 25 · Day 1
Tokyo · arrival · Shinjuku
The plan
Land 5 AM, taxi to the Park Hyatt, drop bags (or check in early if arranged). Keep it light and stay awake until evening.
Easy afternoon: the Odakyu line from Shinjuku reaches Gōtokuji (lucky cats) and Shiro-Hige (Totoro cream puffs) in ~15 min — quiet, low-effort, perfect on no sleep.
Back in Shinjuku: Don Quijote, Omoide Yokochō yakitori alley, the free view from the Metropolitan Government Building. Shodai in Ebisu for dinner if you can get the booking.
New York Bar on the Park Hyatt's 52nd floor for a nightcap — the view is the whole reason to stay here.
Monday, Oct 26 · Day 2
Tokyo · east side
The plan
Check out, drop bags at Hotel Toranomon Hills. Tsukiji breakfast is 10 min away. Then Sensō-ji in Asakusa and teamLab Planets in Toyosu (book a slot).
Both are on the east side of the city, so this day groups naturally.
Tuesday, Oct 27 · Day 3 Decide
Tokyo · west side — or the mountain day
If staying in Tokyo
Harajuku first: Samoyed Café moffu (booked slot), Latte Pocket for the 4D latte, Takeshita Street. Walk 15 min to Shibuya: Zuichō or Katsugyu for lunch, RURU matcha, Don Quijote, Shibuya Sky at sunset (book the ~4:45 PM slot), Hikiniku to Come for dinner if booked.
If Gōtokuji and Shiro-Hige didn't happen on Day 1, they go first thing — same west side of town.
If doing Fuji or Hakone
Option A or B from the Fuji / Hakone card. The Shibuya food then gets squeezed into Day 1 evening or Day 2.
Wednesday, Oct 28 · Day 4
Tokyo → Kyoto
The plan
Bullet train, 2 hr 15 min (or the Hakone overnight, Option C). teamLab Biovortex is a 7-min walk from Kyoto Station — book a late-afternoon slot and do it straight off the train. Evening in Gion and along the river.
Tickets on the Smart-EX app; seats open a month ahead.
Thursday, Oct 29 · Day 5
Kyoto · Higashiyama
The plan
Morning: kimono rental + matcha tasting (book ahead), then the Higashiyama walk in the kimono: Kiyomizu-dera → stone lanes → Hōkan-ji → Gion, with Nishiki Market for a grazing lunch. Return the kimono by late afternoon (shops close ~5–6 PM), K36 at sunset, Yukichi Honpo ramen or Katsugyu after.
Friday, Oct 30 · Day 6
Kyoto · Arashiyama
The plan
Morning Hozugawa boat (train to Kameoka first), ends in Arashiyama → bamboo forest → monkey park. Kinkaku-ji on the way back in the afternoon.
Saturday, Oct 31 · Day 7
Nara day trip
The plan
Deer park + Tōdai-ji in the morning, back in Kyoto by mid-afternoon. It's Halloween on a Saturday — Osaka's Dōtonbori is 15 min away if they want the scene; Shibuya is the one place to avoid.
Sunday, Nov 1 · Day 8 Decide
Open day
The plan
Either a last Kyoto day (anything missed, or Fushimi Inari at dawn) with the bullet train the next morning, or head back to Tokyo tonight for a last Tokyo evening near the airport.
Monday, Nov 2 · Day 9
Home
The plan
6 PM flight. At the airport by 3 PM. From Kyoto that means a bullet train no later than ~10 AM; from a Tokyo hotel it's a slow morning.
