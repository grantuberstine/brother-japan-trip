Fuji / Hakone

Considering one of these · three ways to do it

Pick one, or skip Decide
Mountain day
Option A · Fuji day trip from Tokyo (Day 3)
Kawaguchiko (Fuji Five Lakes) — ~2 hr from Shinjuku by highway bus or the Fuji Excursion train. Chūreitō Pagoda (the pagoda-and-Fuji photo), Lake Kawaguchi, Oishi Park.
Closest, biggest Fuji views. Leave early — the mountain is clearest in the morning and often clouds over by afternoon. Late October is the start of clear-sky season.
Option B · Hakone day trip from Tokyo (Day 3)
Hakone loop — Romancecar from Shinjuku (85 min) or bullet train to Odawara (35 min). Hakone Free Pass covers the mountain train, ropeway over the volcanic valley, pirate ship across Lake Ashi, and the red torii in the water at Hakone Shrine.
More variety than Fuji, hot springs, Fuji in the background if it's clear. Long day: ~10 hours door to door.
Option C · Hakone on the way to Kyoto (Day 4, overnight)
Ship suitcases Tokyo → Kyoto, stop in Hakone for a night at a hot-spring ryokan with dinner, continue to Kyoto next morning (2 hr 10 from Odawara).
Takes one Kyoto night. The most relaxed version, and the only one with an onsen soak.

Day by day

Follow this top to bottom. Times are targets, not rules. TBD = still to pick.

Sunday, Oct 25 · Day 1 Park Hyatt Tokyo
Tokyo · arrival · Shinjuku + the lucky cats
5:00 AM land
Immigration (Visit Japan Web QR), bags, cash from a 7-Eleven ATM in arrivals.
Taxi to the Park Hyatt: ~40 min from Haneda (~¥10,000) or ~80 min from Narita. Or Suica + train if you're feeling fresh.
7:00 – 10:00 AM Park Hyatt Tokyo, Shinjuku
Drop bags (or check in, if early check-in is arranged). Shower if they have a room. Breakfast at the hotel or a conbini.
The room officially starts at 3 PM — the ask for early check-in goes in now, before the trip.
10:30 AM Odakyu line to Gōtokuji · 15 min
Gōtokuji Temple — the lucky-cat temple. Thousands of waving cats behind the main hall. 45 minutes.
Walk 12 min (or the hotel shuttle) to Shinjuku Station → Odakyu line local train → Gōtokuji station → 5-min walk.
12:15 PM Shiro-Hige's Cream Puff Factory, Setagaya-Daita
Shiro-Hige — Totoro cream puffs and coffee. Light lunch here, or hold out for Shinjuku.
Odakyu line 2 stops back toward Shinjuku → Setagaya-Daita station → 5-min walk. Then Odakyu back to Shinjuku, 10 min.
2:00 – 4:00 PM Park Hyatt
Check in. Nap, 90 minutes max, alarm on. Then daylight and a walk — this is what fixes the jet lag.
4:30 PM Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building · 10-min walk
Free observation deck on the 45th floor. Sunset is 4:50 PM. Optional, but it's the easiest view in the city and it's next door.
6:30 PM dinner
Shodai, Ebisu — the potato udon — if the reservation lands. 15-min taxi from the hotel.
Otherwise TBD · dinner in Shinjuku
8:30 PM Shinjuku · Kabukichō
Don Quijote Kabukichō — open 24 hours, 10 min from the hotel. Snacks, gifts, the chaos.
Nightcap at the New York Bar on the Park Hyatt's 52nd floor (cover charge after ~8 PM for the live jazz). In bed by 10:30.
Monday, Oct 26 · Day 2 Hotel Toranomon Hills
Tokyo · east side · temple, market, teamLab
7:30 AM move hotels
Check out of the Park Hyatt. Taxi to Hotel Toranomon Hills (20 min), drop bags at the desk. Room comes at 3 PM.
8:15 AM Ginza line, Toranomon → Asakusa · 25 min direct
Sensō-ji — Kaminarimon gate, the Nakamise street, the temple. At 8:45 it's calm; the stalls open around 9–10 while you're there. 75 minutes.
Toranomon Station is a 5-min walk from the hotel. Ginza line all the way to the last stop, Asakusa.
10:30 AM Tsukiji Outer Market · taxi 20 min
Tsukiji brunch crawl — sushi, tamago on a stick, wagyu skewers, strawberries. Graze 6–8 stalls. Stalls run until ~2 PM.
Taxi from Asakusa is simplest (~¥2,500). Train: Ginza line to Ueno → Hibiya line to Tsukiji, ~30 min.
1:00 PM teamLab Planets, Toyosu · taxi 15 min
teamLab Planets — book the 1:00 or 1:30 slot. Barefoot, water rooms, mirrors. Wear pants you can roll up. 2 hours.
TBD · book the ticket
4:00 PM Hotel Toranomon Hills · taxi 25 min
Check in, rest. The Toranomon Hills towers have a whole food floor downstairs; Ginza is 10 min away.

Hozugawa boat down the gorge — traditional wooden boat, boatmen with poles, light rapids. Ends at the Arashiyama bridge ~11:00.

Iwatayama Monkey Park — 20-min steep climb, macaques at the top, city view. ~1 hour round trip.
3:00 PM Kinkaku-ji · taxi 25 min (~¥3,000)
Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion (¥500) — last entry 5 PM. 40 minutes is plenty.

Saturday, Oct 31 · Day 7 · Halloween
Nara · deer and the Great Buddha
8:30 AM Kintetsu line, Kyoto Station → Kintetsu-Nara · 35–45 min
Limited express is reserved and 35 min (~¥1,300); the regular express is 45 min (~¥760). Either is fine.
9:30 AM Nara Park · 15-min walk from the station
The deer — ¥200 for crackers, they bow, then they mob you. Keep the crackers hidden until you're ready.
10:15 AM Tōdai-ji · in the park
Tōdai-ji (¥800) — the giant bronze Buddha in the world's biggest wooden hall. 45 minutes.
Then the lantern-lined path to Kasuga Taisha if you want another 45 minutes in the park.
