Basics

The few things that apply no matter what the plan ends up being

🇯🇵 Japan
Good to know
Entry
US passports: visa-free up to 90 days. Fill out Visit Japan Web a day or two before flying — immigration becomes a QR scan.
Getting around
Add a Suica card to Apple Wallet before you go — tap for every train, bus and convenience store.
Bullet train tickets on the Smart-EX app (seats open 1 month ahead). Google Maps handles all local transit.
Ship suitcases between cities with Yamato "takkyubin" from any hotel desk — next-day, ~¥2,500–3,000 a bag.
Money & manners
Cards work most places; keep ¥10,000 in cash for small ramen shops, temples and bars. 7-Eleven ATMs take US cards.
No tipping, ever. Shoes off where there's a step up. Quiet on trains. No eating while walking.
Weather · late Oct
Highs ~65–70°F, lows ~52–57°F, sun down by 5 PM. Light jacket, walking shoes, a compact umbrella.
